## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This work session focused on preparing for the first reading of the adopted budget scheduled for the following week. Council President Jamie Dunphy (District 1) facilitated a round-robin discussion allowing each councilor 12 minutes to present potential budget notes and amendments. The meeting revealed significant tensions around workforce reductions, funding sources, and budget priorities as Portland faces ongoing fiscal challenges.

The most substantial proposal came from Councilor Mitch Green (District 4), working with Candace Avalos (District 1, Housing & Permitting Committee Chair) and Angelita Morillo (District 3), who presented a $16.5 million amendment using Portland Clean Energy Fund (PCF) interest to pause core services realignment and preserve 103 city positions [00:11:55]. This proposal sparked extensive debate about fiscal sustainability and the use of one-time funds.

Multiple councilors raised concerns about council office budget cuts, with Steve Novick (District 3) proposing to reduce council budgets by $142,146 each (excluding District 1) to fund Fire Rescue Station 19 [01:37:50]. This led to heated exchanges about equitable treatment of council districts and the appropriate level of legislative branch funding.

The session highlighted deep disagreements about budget priorities, with some councilors advocating for preserving public safety positions while others worried about creating unsustainable fiscal cliffs. The deadline for filing amendments and budget notes with the City Budget Office is 10:00 AM Friday, with final filing to the clerk at 10:00 AM Monday, June 8th.

## 3. TOPIC FLAGS

### Real Estate & Housing

**SDCs Discussion:** Tiffany Koyama Lane (District 3) proposed a technical amendment regarding the Montavilla Park picnic shelter, highlighting a bureaucratic issue where the project doesn't qualify for either parks levy capital funding (for existing assets) or SDC funding (for new assets) due to timing quirks in the SDC methodology [01:15:06]. The City Budget Office recommended using parks general fund temporarily until SDC methodology revisions in FY 27-28.

**Housing Committee Reference:** Discussion touched on immigration services office establishment, with Morillo noting it could be funded without taking from the mayor's office under the Green/Avalos/Morillo proposal.

### System Development Charges (SDCs) & Permitting Fees

The Montavilla Park shelter case revealed gaps in current SDC methodology where assets torn down and rebuilt fall into funding limbo. Ruth Levine (City Budget Director) and staff indicated SDC methodology revisions planned for next year to address such issues.

### City Budget & Finance

**Major Budget Gap:** CFO Jonas Biery (CFO / Deputy City Administrator for Budget & Finance) indicated Portland faces a $75 million deficit next year [00:19:11], raising concerns about using one-time PCF funds.

**PCF Interest Usage:** Green's proposal would use $16.5 million in PCF interest accrued in FY25-26, in addition to the $26 million already approved from FY24-25, totaling $42.5 million in PCF interest spending [00:17:56].

**Contingency Funds:** Loretta Smith (District 1) identified potential funding sources including $138 million in grants fund external materials and services, $53 million in facilities services operating fund contingency, and $42 million in Technology Services Fund [00:44:41].

**Council Office Budgets:** Heated debate over $17.4 million total spending on council offices versus $6.4 million under previous government structure [01:37:50].

### Public Safety

**Fire Rescue Cuts:** Multiple councilors proposed restoring Advanced Life Support (ALS) rescue units being reduced from 24/7 to 12-hour operations, particularly Station 19. Green's proposal includes $1.65 million to restore 2 ALS teams. Sameer Kanal (District 2) and Tiffany Koyama Lane also working on rescue restoration amendments.

**Police Bureau Restoration:** Green's amendment includes restoring 26 FTEs using $3.45 million from Portland Police Bureau PS3 positions. Discussion of victim services units restoration with 2 FTEs at $302,000.

**Emergency Communications:** Kanal proposed restoring one of three positions being eliminated at Bureau of Emergency Communications (BOEC) using funds freed up from a previous amendment.

### Infrastructure & Transportation

Brief mention of Public Works realignment affecting HR and other administrative positions, but limited detail provided during the session.

### Homelessness & Shelter Policy

**Shelter Operations:** Eric Zimmerman (District 4) expressed concerns about closing City Team Shelter on Grand and moving to Pearl District shelter for more money with similar bed count. Also raised issues about expanding North Portland TAS site capacity while maintaining Urban Alchemy contract.

**Urban Alchemy Discussion:** Ryan and Kanal debated Urban Alchemy's performance, with Ryan noting higher success rates in recovery and workforce placement despite operational challenges [02:15:59].
