The resolution before you, document number 2026-203, was heard in the Housing and Permitting Committee on June 16th, where it was referred to full council with a recommendation it be adopted. The resolution urges the city administrator to reestablish a permitting improvement team within the Community and Economic Development Service Area to initiate the second phase of the city's code alignment project, or CAP 2. The resolution requests that the city administrator and the deputy city administrators ensure participation in this effort by all relevant city service areas and bureaus to identify and resolve code and regulatory conflicts with the goal of consolidating the city's permitting authority, reducing regulatory burdens, and encouraging economic development. The resolution further clarifies that the Permitting Improvement Team, at the city administrator's direction, is authorized to serve as the coordinating lead of this work, including convening an external advisory body with stakeholders experienced in design, construction, permitting and development to provide guidance to the team. The city administrator is asked to ensure that all improvements to city administrative rules, procedures, and city code be made in collaboration with council and focus on improvements to encourage economic development. Finally, the resolution requests delivery of the following from the city administrator to the city council: a report within 60 days of adoption of the resolution that outlines the Cap 2 project scope, timeline, staffing, and resource needs; a work plan within 150 days of adoption that identifies a permanent code team responsible for code and administrative rule drafting, a list of city code chapters requiring amendment, and a framework for centralizing development review and permitting under a single city entity, and assessment of the benefits and challenges of this consolidation. And finally, identification of staff and resources needed for this work, as well as quarterly progress reports on CAP 2 improvement work. The full impact statement on this item includes information on economic and real estate development impacts and analysis, and community impacts and community involvement. Two people provided verbal testimony at the committee meeting on June 16th, and two people submitted written testimony prior to the committee's action. The general themes of this testimony that the committee heard included highlighting the increased complexity of the permitting process and adverse impacts to project timelines, recognizing the urgency in removing barriers to develop needed housing of all types, SDCs for all income levels, the need for mechanisms for the city to hold housing developers accountable and ensure compliance with fire life and safety standards, particularly for affordable housing properties, and finally, support for the resolution's emphasis on a single coordinating authority focusing on codes that drive costs and delay and identifying a sustainable funding strategy and centering accountability and stakeholder input. And that concludes the city staff summary— committee staff summary. Thank you.
